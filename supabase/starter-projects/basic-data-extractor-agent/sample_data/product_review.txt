Product Review: The EcoBrew Coffee Maker

This coffee maker is made mostly from recycled materials and cuts
brewing time by almost half compared to our old machine. It has three
brew-strength settings and an auto-shutoff feature that has already
saved us from a couple of forgetful mornings.

The water tank could be a little bigger, and the instructions that came
in the box were confusing, but overall this is a solid choice for anyone
looking to reduce their environmental footprint without sacrificing
their morning routine.
