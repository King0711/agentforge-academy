# Golden Crust Bakery - Frequently Asked Questions

## What are your opening hours?
We're open Tuesday to Sunday, 8:00 AM to 7:00 PM. We're closed every Monday for baking prep and stock-taking.

## Where are you located?
We're at 14 Adeniran Ogunsanya Street, Surulere, Lagos. We don't have a second branch yet.

## Do you deliver?
Yes, we deliver within Lagos mainland for a flat NGN 1,500 fee. Orders placed before 12 PM are usually delivered the same day; anything placed after that goes out the next day.

## How far in advance do I need to order a custom cake?
Custom and celebration cakes need at least 48 hours' notice. Wedding cakes and orders above 5kg need at least 7 days' notice so we can plan tiers and delivery properly.

## What payment methods do you accept?
We accept bank transfer, POS on delivery or pickup, and cash. We don't currently accept cheques.

## Can you cater for allergies or dietary needs?
We offer an eggless option for most cakes and a gluten-free vanilla sponge on request. We are NOT a nut-free kitchen - all our cakes are made in the same space as products containing groundnuts and cashews, so we can't guarantee zero cross-contact.

## What sizes and prices do your cakes come in?
Our round cakes start at NGN 8,000 for a 6-inch (serves 8-10) and go up to NGN 35,000 for a 12-inch (serves 40-50). Custom designs and fondant work are quoted separately based on the design.

## Can I cancel or change my order after paying?
Orders can be changed or cancelled for a full refund up to 24 hours before your pickup or delivery time. Inside 24 hours, we can only offer a 50% refund since ingredients are already prepped.

## Do you do wholesale or bulk orders for events and offices?
Yes - for orders of 20 units or more (small chops, cupcakes, pastries) we offer a 10% discount and can discuss a standing weekly order for offices. Reach out at least a week ahead.

## How do I get in touch with a real person?
Call or WhatsApp us on 0803-000-0000 between 8 AM and 7 PM, Tuesday to Sunday, or email goldencrustlagos@example.com.
